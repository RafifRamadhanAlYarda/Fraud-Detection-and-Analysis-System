# Project Guidelines & Agent Instructions

## Naming & Branding Constraints
- **Strictly No BRI/Brimo Branding**: The application must never contain any reference to "BRI" or "Brimo" in its visual labels, headers, descriptions, or GUI screens.
- **Generic Terminology**:
  - Replace "Bank BRI" with generic terms like "Enterprise Bank", "BUMN Bank", or "FDAS Portal".
  - Replace "BRILink" with "Banking Agent" or "Mitra Agent".
  - Replace "Brimo" with "Mobile Banking App".
  - Replace "BRIVA" with "Virtual Account (VA)".
  - Use generic bank building vector icons rather than specific proprietary logos.

## Color Palette
- **Primary Brand Color**: Deep Navy & Signature Brand Blue (`#00529C` brand accent and `#021B33` to `#00529C` gradients) to keep the forensic theme cohesive.
